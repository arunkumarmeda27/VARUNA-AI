Where VARUNA-AI Improves Forecasts

  • Monsoon Lows & Depressions: Resolves NWP extreme convective under-prediction by coupling 850 hPa vorticity and moisture flux convergence indices.
  • Active Monsoon Orographic Slopes: Recovers windward precipitation deficit along the Western Ghats and Northeast hills using the orographic flux component.
  • Break Monsoon Spurious Rain: Suppresses NWP drizzle false alarms across central Indian plains when the monsoon trough is displaced north to the foothills.

Identified Scientific Limitations & Failure Modes

  • Rapid Synoptic Transitions: Transient under-prediction occurs during rapid un-forecasted Western Disturbance intrusions over Northwest India.
  • Localized Sub-Grid Cloudbursts: Local convective phenomena (>250 mm/hour) remain difficult to predict deterministically without meso-scale radar data.
  • Missing Observation Latency: Offline fallback modes are triggered if real-time ground telemetry data is delayed.
