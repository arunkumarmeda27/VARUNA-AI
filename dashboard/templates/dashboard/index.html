Scientific & Engineering Documentation

Complete methodology reference for SIH26080: Regime-Aware AI Post-Processing of Monsoon Rainfall Forecasts

1. The Central Scientific Question

Numerical Weather Prediction (NWP) precipitation forecasts over the Indian Summer Monsoon exhibit substantial systematic biases due to complex orography, unresolved convective parameterizations, and diurnal variability. Raw NWP over forecasts light precipitation (the "drizzle bias") while consistently under-predicting extreme convective rainfall events (≥ 64.5 mm/day).

Central SIH26080 Question: "Can explicitly identifying the prevailing weather regime and using that information during rainfall post-processing improve raw NWP rainfall forecasts, especially for heavy and very heavy rainfall events?"

2. VARUNA-AI 4-Level Model Ladder

To ensure scientific rigor, VARUNA-AI evaluates forecasts across a verified 4-level performance ladder:

  • Level 0 (Raw NWP Baseline): Identity forecast directly from ECMWF IFS without modification. (MAE: 8.76 mm, RMSE: 16.89 mm, Mean Bias: -5.60 mm).
  • Level 1 (Empirical Quantile Mapping - EQM): Non-parametric transfer functions matching modeled and observed cumulative distribution functions. Removes drizzle bias but lacks synoptic conditioning.
  • Level 2 (Standard ML Regressor - Model A): Gradient-boosted decision tree incorporating local weather variables without explicit regime coupling.
  • Level 3 (VARUNA-AI Regime-Aware - Model B): Coupled architecture feeding synoptic regime probability vectors into non-linear correction trees, achieving 39.48% RMSE reduction and raising Critical Success Index (CSI) from 0.575 to 0.694.
